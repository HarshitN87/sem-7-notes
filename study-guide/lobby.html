The shape of the examination

Three real mid-semester papers were supplied: TCS-704 Advanced Computer Architecture (Oct 2022 and Oct 2023) and TIT-721 Business Intelligence (Oct 2023). All three follow the same format, and every theory paper in this Almanac uses it:

B. Tech. (CSE) (Seventh Semester) · Mid Semester Examination
Time: 1½ Hours · Maximum Marks: 50
Note: (i) Answer all the questions by choosing any one of the sub-questions. (ii) Each sub-question carries 10 marks.

So each paper has five questions, each with an internal choice (a) OR (b), and each worth 10 marks. There is no short-answer section. Every answer you write is a 10-mark answer, and you have about 18 minutes per answer. That is why every topic here follows the same six-part structure: definition, first principles, mechanism, real-world case, critical evaluation, and the evaluator's checklist. Learn that structure and you can write a full-length answer on any of these topics.

The questions on each paper are tagged with the course outcome they test (CO1 for Unit 1, CO2 for Unit 2, and so on). In the ACA papers Q1–Q3 are mostly Unit 1 and Q3(b)–Q5 are Unit 2. The BI paper follows the same pattern: CO1 for Q1–Q3(a), CO2 for Q3(b)–Q5(a), and CO3 for Q5(b). The expected-question papers in this guide copy that split.

A note on the sourcesThe file named TCS726 2023 pyq.pdf is a Business Intelligence paper (TIT-721), not Research Methodology. No Research Methodology & IPR paper was supplied. The RM & IPR questions in this guide are written from your professor's notes and use the same 5 × (a/b) format. The ACA and BI questions reproduce and extend the actual paper questions, marked PYQ 2022 or PYQ 2023.

How to use this Almanac

  • First read (the night before each exam): go through the guide for that subject. The Evaluator's Checklist box in each topic lists the terms, formulas and diagrams an examiner looks for.
  • Active recall: each unit ends with a flashcard deck. Tap a card to turn it over. Use Got it and Again to move cards between three Leitner boxes, then use Review weakest first. Decks in Unit 2 and Unit 3 include cumulative cards (marked ↺) that go back over earlier units.
  • Exam simulation: each theory subject ends with a full expected paper. Try each question with a timer (18 minutes), then open the model answer blueprint and compare it with yours.
  • MBA-302 is 100% multiple choice, so its guide is a scored question bank. Each question has four options, and each explanation says why the right answer is right and why the others are wrong.
  • Diagrams: every plate has a How to sketch line, which is the version you can draw in about 90 seconds in the exam hall.
  • Getting around: each subject has its own page. Use the ticket bar at the top to jump between subjects, the unit buttons under each subject's title to jump to a unit, and the links at the bottom of each page to go to the previous or next subject. Each page remembers where you stopped reading.
  • Printing: open a subject's page and use your browser's print command. The print styles open every model answer and list every flashcard.
